<p>This Privacy Policy explains how <b>[YOUR NAME / STUDIO NAME]</b> ("we", "us") handles information when you play the mobile game <b>Heist Duo</b> ("the game").</p>

<h2>1. Information stored on your device</h2>
<p>The game saves your progress (unlocked levels, stars, coins, selected character skin and sound setting) on your device only. We do not run our own servers and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number or account details through the game.</p>

<h2>2. Advertising (Google AdMob)</h2>
<p>The game shows ads using <b>Google AdMob</b>: banner ads on menu screens and optional rewarded video ads (for example, to earn bonus coins or continue a level). To show ads, Google and its partners may collect and use certain information from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and information about how you interact with ads. This data is handled by Google under its own policies:</p>
<ul>
 <li><a href="https://policies.google.com/privacy">Google Privacy Policy</a></li>
 <li><a href="https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites">How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services</a></li>
</ul>
<p>You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your device settings (Settings &rarr; Google &rarr; Ads).</p>

<h2>3. Consent in the EEA, UK and Switzerland</h2>
<p>If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows a consent message provided by Google (User Messaging Platform) before ads are requested. You can change your choice later using the "AD PRIVACY" button on the home screen, when it is available.</p>

<h2>4. Children</h2>
<p>Heist Duo is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.</p>

<h2>5. Data sharing and security</h2>
<p>We do not sell your personal information. Information used for ads is processed by Google as described above. Game progress stays on your device; uninstalling the game removes it.</p>

<h2>6. Changes to this policy</h2>
